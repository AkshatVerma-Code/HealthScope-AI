Clinical Frameworks

Medical diagnostic streams

We specialize in two distinct branches of diagnostic analysis, keeping the boundaries and accepted standards fully clear.

Blood Biomarker Suite

Our deep-parse engine maps blood chemistry results against strict physiological cohorts to isolate underlying wellness biomarkers.

Supported Lab Inputs
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC, Hemoglobin, WBC)
  • Metabolic & Liver Function Panels (ALT, AST, ALP)
  • Lipid Cohorts (LDL, HDL, Triglycerides)
  • Kidney Indicators (eGFR, Creatinine, Urea)

Radiology & Imaging Scope

Our neural vision models analyze cross-sectional radiology scans to highlight tissue structures and cross-reference findings.

Supported Diagnostic Scans
  • Brain MRI & CT (structural tissue classification)
  • Spine, Joints & Bone Scans (density, wear)
  • Verifiable raw DICOM formats & medical PDFs
